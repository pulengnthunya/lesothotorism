Explore Our Tourism Services

Lesotho offers many tourism experiences for visitors. Explore our services to discover historical places, Basotho traditions, beautiful landscapes, outdoor adventures and travel options throughout the country.

Whether you are visiting for education, relaxation, adventure or cultural experiences, Lesotho has many places for you to discover.

1. Guided Tours and Historical Sites

Explore important historical places such as Thaba Bosiu and Morija. Learn about King Moshoeshoe I and the history of the Basotho Nation.

Historical tours can help visitors understand Lesotho's past, heritage and important historical events. Check tour availability and opening hours before visiting.

2. Basotho Cultural Experiences

Discover Basotho traditions, traditional clothing, music, crafts, food and stories from communities across Lesotho.

Visitors can learn about the Basotho blanket, mokorotlo hat and local customs. Ask local tourism offices about available cultural activities.

3. Hiking and Outdoor Adventures

Enjoy mountain walks, hiking, pony trekking and outdoor adventures in different parts of the Mountain Kingdom.

Outdoor activities depend on the destination, weather and local conditions. Wear suitable clothing and footwear, and follow safety advice.

4. Sightseeing and Photography

Visit mountain landscapes, valleys, waterfalls, dams and scenic viewpoints while enjoying the natural beauty of Lesotho.

Visitors can explore places such as Maletsunyane Falls and Katse Dam. Plan your route, check road conditions and respect local site rules.

5. Educational and Group Visits

Schools, colleges and groups can explore Lesotho's history, geography, culture and natural environment.

Teachers and group organisers should contact the relevant attraction or tour provider to ask about group visits, educational activities, prices and availability.

6. Accommodation and Transport

Find accommodation and transport options for trips to Maseru, the highlands and other destinations across Lesotho.

Compare hotels, guesthouses and lodges by location, price and facilities. Check transport arrangements and confirm availability before travelling.
